The bar chart starts at zero and uses same-unit values. Three categories are sufficient because each is an intentionally distinct audited financial measure; a trend chart would be underpowered and would answer a different question.

No chart is used for competitor fees because platform fee is not comparable without processing, taxes, payout, reserve, dispute, and content-policy economics. Exact product sequencing is presented as a table because phase and launch condition are lookup fields, not a quantitative ranking claim.

The Fenix filing is primary evidence. Public account counts are intentionally omitted from the chart because combining hundreds of millions of fan accounts with a few million creator accounts would obscure interpretation and still not provide activity denominators.

The report is product research, not a legal opinion. Missing vendor contracts, underwriting, jurisdiction decisions, and proprietary retention/economics data are visible caveats rather than access issues because the snapshot is ready for its stated strategic purpose.
